# UI audit, 2026-11-27 (round 10)

Checked start, levels, play, menu, controls, customise at 390x844 and
play at 1280x800.

## Changed

- Drag handle: soft line from the pivot to the finger, a pivot dot and
  two curved arrows (clockwise, counter-clockwise) around the ring. The
  arrow for the current turning direction lights up. Fades in and out in
  200 ms, uses the accent colour so it follows every look.
- Controls (menu > Controls): Drag, tap and double-tap, tap and hold.
  Saved in settings. The press shows a dashed focus frame once the hold
  time has passed. The single tap only waits out the 250 ms window in
  the double-tap mode.
- Customize (menu > Customize, also the start-screen button): four
  pickers (white balls, black balls, board, background), six options
  each, drawn as live ball swatches and mini boards. Applies at once,
  saved, Reset button. Options are plain data in themes.js and may carry
  an `unlock` field later; the menu already filters on isUnlocked().
- Motion: pages fade and slide in (200 ms), sheets fade in with the card
  rising, buttons press down and show a focus ring, hover tint.
- Solve: the board pops slightly, balls pulse, the result sheet waits
  650 ms instead of 250 so the animation is seen.
- Help text mentions the Controls setting.

## Would still improve

- Levels page is empty below the first chapter; the Practice button
  could sit at the bottom, and chapters could be cards.
- Page change is a fade only; a direction-aware slide (forward/back)
  would feel more native.
- Swatch grids could show the real board with the chosen balls in one
  live preview at the top of the Customize sheet.
- No sound or haptic on solve yet (backlog).
- Hold mode has no visible progress ring while holding; only the frame
  turns dashed.
- Locked options should be shown greyed with the unlock hint once
  progression exists.
